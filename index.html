How it works

  1. 1

    Play three quests

    Walk the Lantern Road, enter the Silent Forest and cross the Drowned Island. Each quest asks you to choose — there are no right or wrong answers.

  2. 2

    Choices become clues

    Every choice carries gentle themes like rest, connection or uncertainty. A simple, visible rule set turns them into a personal reflection.

  3. 3

    Restore the Light

    A guided breathing garden. Follow a glowing orb at a comfortable pace and watch the garden brighten.

  4. 4

    Keep what matters

    Save journeys, log a mood and write journal entries. They stay only in this browser — nothing is ever sent anywhere.

    About & Safety

    A gentle mirror, not a diagnosis

    What MindQuest is

    A short fantasy adventure that invites you to notice how you respond to slow progress, distance from others and competing priorities. Your choices map to broad themes through simple rules you can inspect at the end of every journey.

    What it is not

    MindQuest does not diagnose anxiety, depression or any other condition, and game choices cannot reliably identify mental illness. It is not therapy and not a substitute for professional care.

    Privacy

    No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Anything you save is stored only in this browser and can be erased with “Clear all data”.

    If you need support

    If you are in distress or feel unsafe, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional. In India you can call Tele-MANAS at 14416 (free, 24×7). In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.
